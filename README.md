## 📊 Project Overview

The **Apex Logistics Operational Performance Dashboard** is an interactive business intelligence project developed in Microsoft Power BI to monitor and analyze the financial and operational performance of a logistics company.

The dashboard transforms transportation data into meaningful insights, helping users understand revenue trends, cost distribution, profitability, and performance across different business dimensions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Monitor key financial and operational performance indicators.
- Analyze monthly transportation revenue trends.
- Compare revenue performance across companies.
- Evaluate net profit across different cities and transportation types.
- Explore business performance through interactive filters.

## 🔑 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| Total Transportation Revenue | Overall revenue generated from transportation services |
| Total Cost | Total cost associated with transportation operations |
| Net Profit | Revenue remaining after costs |
| Profit Margin (%) | Percentage of revenue retained as profit |

## 📊 Dashboard Features

**1. Monthly Revenue Analysis**
- Visualizes transportation revenue by month.
- Helps identify revenue trends and monthly fluctuations.

**2. Company Performance Analysis**
- Compares transportation revenue across companies.
- Highlights the highest revenue-generating business partners.

**3. City-Based Profitability Analysis**
- Analyzes net profit across different cities.
- Provides a breakdown by transportation type.

**4. Interactive Filters**
- Date Range
- Customer Segment (Individual, Corporate, Wholesale)
- Transportation Type

These filters allow users to explore the dashboard from different business perspectives.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** — Dashboard development and data visualization.
- **Power BI Interactive Filters** — Dynamic data exploration.

## 💡 Business Value

This dashboard provides a consolidated view of logistics performance and supports data-driven decision-making by making revenue, costs, and profitability easier to monitor and compare.
